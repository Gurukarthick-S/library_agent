Group Study Rooms
Source: written for this sample project (imaginary library).

Rooms
There are 6 group study rooms on the third floor. Each room seats 4 to 8 people and has a whiteboard.

Booking
Rooms are booked on the library portal, up to 3 days ahead. A booking lasts at most 2 hours per group per day. A group needs at least 3 members.

Check-in
The group must check in at the third-floor desk within 15 minutes of the start time. After 15 minutes the booking is cancelled and the room is given to others.

Rules for rooms
Rooms may be used between 9:00 am and 7:00 pm. Leave the whiteboard clean and the furniture in place. Repeated no-shows lead to a one-week booking ban.
