Campus Library: General Rules
Source: written for this sample project (imaginary library).

Opening hours
The library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, and Saturday from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. It is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

Who can use the library
Students, faculty and staff can enter with a valid campus ID card. Visitors may use the reading hall for one day with a written pass from the front desk.

Quiet and behaviour
Silence is required on the first and second floors. Phone calls are allowed only in the entrance lobby. Food is not allowed inside; water in a closed bottle is permitted.

Computers and printing
There are 20 computers on the ground floor for research and study. Printing costs Rs 2 per page for black and white. Computers may be used for up to 2 hours at a time when others are waiting.

Lost ID cards
If you lose your ID card, report it to the front desk. A temporary entry slip is given for 3 days.
